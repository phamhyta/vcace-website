VCACE is an emerging Australia-based bilateral cultural initiative connecting people, ideas and institutions through cultural experiences, dialogue, education and exchange.

Culture connects people, and creates new possibilities.

We envision a stronger Australia–Vietnam relationship enriched by shared cultural understanding, creative collaboration and lasting people-to-people connections. VCACE works across contemporary arts, music, film, performing arts, food, heritage, cultural education and living traditions.

Through partnerships, we create spaces to experience culture, exchange perspectives and develop work that crosses borders. We bring together artists, educators, communities and institutions to curate public experiences, facilitate dialogue, develop learning opportunities and connect collaborators across both countries.

Mutual understanding

We make room for Australian and Vietnamese perspectives and genuine two-way learning.

Collaboration

We create with practitioners and partners, not simply for audiences.

We connect living traditions with today’s ideas, identities and creative practices.

Inclusion

Cultural Encounters — Australia × Vietnam

A proposed inaugural gathering weaving contemporary visual arts, live performance and cultural dialogue into one shared experience.

VCACE is developing its first cultural showcase in Brisbane, bringing together Australia- and Vietnam-connected practitioners for a curated exhibition, an artist-led conversation and an intimate performance. The project aims to create new connections and the foundations for future bilateral collaboration.

Proposed project. Artists, venue, dates, funding and partners are still to be confirmed.
